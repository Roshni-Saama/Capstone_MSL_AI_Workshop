# MSL Role and Reporting Evidence Note

Recorded 13 October 2026. This is a paraphrased research note, not a full article copy.

Source: Theron et al., Promoting Best Practices for Medical Science Liaisons Position Statement from the APPA, IFAPP, MAPS and MSLS. Published 8 July 2021. [Publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43441-021-00310-y). DOI: 10.1007/s43441-021-00310-y.

## Role and relationships

Sections reviewed: Introduction; What is the Role of the MSL; What Are the Responsibilities of MSLs; What is Meant by Non-promotional Activities and Interactions and its Key Principles.

The paper places MSLs in Medical Affairs and recommends reporting into that function to protect scientific autonomy. Responsibilities include scientific exchange, evidence-generation support, and field insight gathering. External relationships include clinical experts and investigators. MSL activities should remain independent of sales targets. Safety information should be submitted through company pharmacovigilance procedures.

## Limits

This is professional guidance, not a company organisation chart or approved policy. It does not establish a universal sequence of management titles. Local procedures and actual reporting lines require company validation.
