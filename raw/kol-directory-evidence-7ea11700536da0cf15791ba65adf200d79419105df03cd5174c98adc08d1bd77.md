# KOL Directory Research Notes

Recorded 13 October 2026. Paraphrased source notes; not full source copies.

## Selection and first contact

Theron et al. (2021), Responsibilities and Scientific Exchange: scientific expertise, publications, research, and society or guideline contributions inform expert identification. Introductory meetings can establish scientific needs and interest. Selection should not depend on prescribing potential. [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43441-021-00310-y).

## Expert profiles and identity

Veeva Link Key People describes curated profiles covering publications, trials, events, associations, and digital activity, accessible through applications and CRM connectors. [Product page](https://www.veeva.com/ap/products/veeva-link/).

IQVIA Expert Ecosystem describes expert profiles linked to CRM or master-data systems and OneKey identifiers connecting datasets. [Product page](https://www.iqvia.com/solutions/real-world-evidence/health-data-apps-and-ai/expert-ecosystem).

## Interpretation limits

Vendor pages describe available capabilities, not adoption by the capstone company. Category tables, therapy mappings, outreach steps, and directory maintenance below are working frameworks requiring company validation. Digital reach does not by itself establish scientific expertise.
