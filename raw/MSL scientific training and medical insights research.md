## Medical insights process

MAPS, Building Medical Insights Capabilities in Medical Affairs Organizations: page 3 discusses fragmented insight practices; page 5 describes collection, documentation, analysis, integration, sharing, and action; page 10 recommends clear definitions, responsibilities, processes, and suitable technology. [White paper PDF](https://medicalaffairs.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/00-MAPS-WP-Building-Medical-Insights-V01-1.pdf).

The findings draw on MAPS membership surveys conducted in 2020. They do not establish the workflow or current difficulties of the capstone company. Company-specific validation is pending.
