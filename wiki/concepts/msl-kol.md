## MSL structure

### Position within the company

MSLs belong to Medical Affairs. Professional guidance recommends reporting into Medical Affairs to preserve scientific independence; collaboration with commercial colleagues does not make sales the reporting function. Source: [Role and relationships](../../raw/msl-role-evidence-1c04fa0376b0109331c0c11a441810e9cf9d7a946d01756a5de9e13cc388c615.md#role-and-relationships).

The following is an illustrative arrangement for discussion, not a validated company chart. Titles, management layers, and reporting lines need confirmation.

```text
Medical Affairs leadership
    |
Field Medical or MSL team leadership
    |
Medical Science Liaison
    <--> KOLs, clinicians, and researchers
          Scientific exchange, not a reporting relationship
```

The MSL is the person conducting scientific engagements. The team leader and Medical Affairs leadership are roles to identify during discovery; their exact responsibilities and authority must be obtained from the company.

### Three parts of the role

The professional guidance groups MSL responsibilities into scientific exchange, support for evidence generation, and insight gathering. Source: [Role and relationships](../../raw/msl-role-evidence-1c04fa0376b0109331c0c11a441810e9cf9d7a946d01756a5de9e13cc388c615.md#role-and-relationships).

For our analysis, this means asking what scientific questions the MSL addresses, what research support is authorised, and how field observations reach the appropriate internal team. Research support should not be interpreted as authority to approve studies or funding.

### Reporting and collaboration

This is a proposed relationship map to validate with the manager. It separates accountability from working relationships.

| Relationship | What to clarify for the capstone | Current status |

### What this means for the AI project

Proposed design implication: distinguish the MSL who prepares and edits a record, the person authorised to review an output, and the team receiving a follow-up task. These may be different people. An organisation chart alone does not establish approval permissions.

Before designing access or routing, establish the actual team structure, review authority, and available information. Selection of a disease area, territory, or product for the prototype remains open.
